Landing-gear sourcing involves safety-critical assemblies and components exposed to substantial mechanical loads. Exact configuration, approved maintenance data and traceability are essential when reviewing a potential replacement source.

A practical review should confirm the aircraft model and gear position, part-number applicability, overhaul status, certification paperwork and any life-limit or inspection requirements.

The references below are independently published pages associated with this topic area, including terminology related to engine, military, distribut, obsolete. Follow each source directly and verify its current information with the original publisher.
