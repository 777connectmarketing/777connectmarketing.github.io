Aircraft repair and overhaul sourcing is closely tied to maintenance data, approved capabilities and the condition of the component being serviced. A repair-station reference should be evaluated against the specific work scope.

Confirm ratings and approvals, capability for the component type, inspection and test requirements, turnaround expectations and the records that will accompany the returned article.
