Supporting Original Equipment Needs OEM | Aviation Parts Reference

Original-equipment and manufacturer references help establish the design origin and technical identity of an aircraft component. The manufacturer's complete part number and revision information are important when checking applicability.

Where an alternate source is considered, verify documented interchangeability or approved supersession rather than assuming that dimensional similarity makes two items equivalent.

The references below are independently published pages associated with this topic area, including terminology related to supporting, original, equipment, needs. Follow each source directly and verify its current information with the original publisher.

Technical sourcing checks

  • Complete part-number and application match
  • Condition, traceability and supporting documentation
