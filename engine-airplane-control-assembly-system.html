Aircraft engine supply chains cover a wide range of assemblies, accessories and replacement components. The correct identification depends on engine model, dash number, modification status and the applicable illustrated parts catalogue.

Procurement checks should include eligibility for the intended engine, condition and release documentation, traceability, shelf-life limits where applicable and confirmation from the responsible maintenance organization.
